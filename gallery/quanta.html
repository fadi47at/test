FAQ

The honest answers

Do I need any experience to start?

No. About 40% of our courses are beginner-friendly and assume nothing. We mark every course with its level so you know before you start.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes — on Pro and Teams plans. We don't put our logo on the certificate (weird, we know) but employers recognize them.

What if I don't like a course?

14-day refund, no questions asked. We'd rather you leave happy than stay frustrated.

Are the instructors actually good?

Every instructor is a working professional in the field they teach. No course-mill teachers. You can see their bio and day job on every course page.

Can I download lessons?

Yes, on Pro and Teams. Watch on a plane, on a train, on a desert island.
